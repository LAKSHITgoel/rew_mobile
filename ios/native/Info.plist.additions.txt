Merge into the app's ios/Runner/Info.plist (inside the top-level <dict>):

  <key>NSMicrophoneUsageDescription</key>
  <string>Used to measure your car's sound with the UMIK-1 microphone.</string>

Registration: if RewAudioPlugin.swift is not part of an auto-registered Flutter
plugin, register the channel in AppDelegate.application(_:didFinishLaunchingWithOptions:)
after GeneratedPluginRegistrant.register(with: self):

    RewAudioPlugin.register(
        with: self.registrar(forPlugin: "RewAudioPlugin")!)

Notes:
  * The UMIK-1 connects via a USB-C / Lightning camera adapter; no special entitlement
    is required beyond the microphone usage string.
  * To keep measuring with the screen active, no background audio mode is needed; add
    the "audio" UIBackgroundModes only if you later measure with the app backgrounded.
